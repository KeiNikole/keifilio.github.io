# Serenity

Serenity is a modern, fully responsive website designed for a beauty salon and spa. This site is crafted to provide visitors with a seamless and relaxing online experience, reflecting the tranquil atmosphere of the salon itself. Serenity aims to showcase the salon’s unique philosophy, highlight its premium services, and make booking appointments effortless for clients.

Features

Navigation Bar
Dropdown Menu: Effortlessly access all key sections of the site with an intuitive dropdown menu.

Interactive Highlights: Menu items feature a smooth gradient highlight on hover, clearly indicating the active selection and enhancing user engagement.

Hero Section
Spa Introduction: Welcomes visitors with a captivating description of the spa and its core philosophy, setting the tone for a soothing experience.

Content Section
Service Carousel: Visually engaging carousel that showcases the salon’s range of luxurious services.

Pricing Table: Transparent and neatly organized pricing table for easy reference.

Booking System: Streamlined booking interface, allowing clients to schedule appointments quickly and conveniently.

Footer
Contact Information: Comprehensive details including email, phone number, and physical address.

Social Media Links: Direct access to the salon’s social media profiles, encouraging clients to connect and stay updated.

Serenity combines elegant design with practical functionality, ensuring every visitor enjoys a beautiful and intuitive browsing experience.
